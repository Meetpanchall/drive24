## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, structured, and modern digital platform for buying, selling, financing, and managing verified pre-owned automobiles. The user experience addresses significant consumer anxiety in high-value online transactions by emphasizing operational transparency, technical precision, and certified vehicle inspections.

The design movement is **Corporate Modern with High-Density Utility**:
- **Clarity over decorative excess:** Clean slate surfaces, precise border structures, and functional data typography replace superficial gradients.
- **Trust architecture:** Verified badges, multi-point inspection summaries, clear ownership histories, and upfront Indian Rupee (₹ Lakh / Crore) pricing models are elevated to primary visual anchors.
- **Dual-role coherence:** Seamless parity between high-converting customer discovery surfaces (car search, vehicle detail pages, financing calculators) and high-density, analytical operations (inventory ingestion, inspection workflows, lead stages, order fulfillment).

## Colors

The color system is engineered for WCAG AAA/AA compliance across dense automotive tables and customer-facing catalogs.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#2563EB` | Hover `#1D4ED8`):** Applied to primary actions, key call-to-action buttons ("Book Test Drive", "Calculate EMI", "Add New Vehicle"), selected filter pills, and active navigation indicators.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` | Elevated `#1E293B`):** Deep Navy foundation. Used for high-contrast headers, admin sidebar navigation, critical dark-mode sections, and high-emphasis typography.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#38BDF8`):** Sky Blue accent for active progress tracks, verification checkmarks, focus rings, and high-visibility highlights.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Page Background: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
  - Card & Modal Surface: `#FFFFFF`
  - Subtle Neutral Fill: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100)
  - Structural Borders: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200)

### Feedback & Status Tiers
- **Success (`#10B981` | Background `#ECFDF5` | Text `#065F46`):** "Passed 150-point inspection", "Delivered", "Verified Seller", and positive margin trends.
- **Warning (`#F59E0B` | Background `#FFFBEB` | Text `#92400E`):** "Pending Inspection", "Under Review", and auction expirations.
- **Danger (`#EF4444` | Background `#FEF2F2` | Text `#991B1B`):** "Rejected", "Cancelled Order", "Critical Inspection Failure", and delete actions.

## Typography

Inter is specified universally across all tiers for its mechanical clarity, tabular numeral support (`tnum`), and balanced x-height.

### Metric and Financial Representation
- **Indian Currency Notation:** All financial values must leverage standard Indian comma separation (e.g., `₹ 12,50,000` or `₹ 6.80 Lakh`) with `price-lg` or `price-md`.
- **Tabular Figures:** For comparison matrices, EMI calculators, odometer readouts (`45,000 km`), and admin data grids, apply the OpenType feature `font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an **8px base grid unit** (`0.5rem = 8px`) ensuring clean alignment between marketplace cards, filter controls, and administrative data tables.

### Grid Implementations
- **Marketplace Customer Catalog (Max Width 1280px):**
  - Desktop: 12-column grid. Left filter rail spans 3 columns (sticky); listing cards span 9 columns (displaying 2 or 3 responsive sub-cards per row).
  - Tablet (768px – 1023px): Filter moves into an off-canvas drawer; card listings reflow to 2 columns.
  - Mobile (<768px): Single-column card stack with horizontal swipeable chips for quick facets (Make, Fuel, Budget, Transmission).
- **Administrative Portal:**
  - Fixed left sidebar at `260px` (or collapsed at `72px`).
  - Fluid main content panel spanning remaining viewport width with `1.5rem` internal page padding.
  - KPI Stat Grid: 4-column layout on desktop, collapsing to 2-column on tablet and 1-column on mobile.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp border boundaries augmented by subtle, cool-toned ambient shadows to maintain enterprise cleanliness and vehicle photo clarity.

### Hierarchy Levels
- **Base Level (Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` flat canvas.
- **Card & Surface (`shadow-sm`):** White container backed by `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` and enclosed with a border of `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Card Hover & Focus (`shadow-md`):** Triggered on vehicle card hover, elevating the card with a `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` drop shadow and a border shift to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Modals, Flyouts & Filter Drawers (`shadow-xl`):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` over a backdrop fill of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.5)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a roundedness tier of **2** (`rounded-lg: 0.75rem / 12px`, `rounded-xl: 1rem / 16px`) delivering a modern, friendly consumer interface while maintaining functional order.

- **Cards & Data Panels:** `1rem` (`rounded-xl`) outer boundary, clipping image thumbnails neatly at top borders.
- **Form Controls & Search Bars:** `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`) to `0.75rem` (`rounded-lg`) for input fields, text areas, and filter selects.
- **Badges, Status Chips, & Filter Pills:** `9999px` (Pill/Full round) for tags such as "150-Point Inspection Passed", "Petrol", "First Owner", and location tags ("Bengaluru", "Mumbai").
- **Admin Tables:** `0.75rem` outer container radius with sharp interior row cells.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#2563EB` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.75rem` border-radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. Hover transition to `#1D4ED8`. Active state scales to `98%`.
- **Secondary:** Slate outline `1px solid #CBD5E1`, `#0F172A` text, hover background `#F1F5F9`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No border, transparent background, `#2563EB` text; background switches to `#EFF6FF` on hover.

### Vehicle Card
- **Structure:** White surface, `1rem` radius, `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Image Module:** Aspect ratio `16:10`, top-corner badges (e.g., "Verified" green badge on top left, wishlist heart icon button on top right with white circular container).
- **Metadata Stack:** Vehicle Title (`title-md`, `#0F172A`), spec row with dot dividers (`Year • Fuel • Odometer` in `body-sm`, `#64748B`), and bottom row anchoring the Price (`price-md`, `#0F172A`) opposite a compact "View Details" button.

### Form Inputs & Search Matrix
- **Global Search Bar:** 4-segment composite selector (Make/Model, Location, Budget, Search Button) wrapped in a single card container with subtle vertical dividers (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Standard Inputs:** Height `44px`, `0.5rem` radius, `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text. Focus state provides an explicit `2px solid #2563EB` ring with a `2px` offset.

### Badges & Inspection Chips
- **Inspection Status:** Horizontal flex layout, `4px` horizontal gap, `0.25rem 0.625rem` padding, pill shape. Success (`#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` label) paired with a green checkmark icon.
- **Specification Chips:** Light slate `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text, `0.75rem` font size, `0.25rem 0.5rem` padding.

### Data Tables (Admin Portal)
- **Header:** `#F8FAFC` background, uppercase tracking (`0.05em`), `12px` font size, bold `#475569` text, `12px 16px` padding.
- **Rows:** `#FFFFFF` background, bottom border `1px solid #F1F5F9`, alternating hover color `#F8FAFC`. Tabular numbers for quantities, price totals, and booking references.
